# Villa Luma — VendeClip video kit

An entirely fictional demonstration property and AI-generated fictional broker. These images were created from new prompts, without using the Bedford Road photos or another real listing as references. The specifications and price below are invented creative inputs, not verified property facts. This is not an offer for sale.

## Files to upload

- `property-photos/`: 10 separate landscape PNG photos, numbered in tour order. Upload these as property photos.
- `presenter/11-broker.png`: a separate portrait of the fictional broker Alex Morgan. Use it as the presenter reference rather than a property scene.
- `listing-en.txt` and `listing-es.txt`: property details and description to copy into your project.
- `VIDEO-SCRIPT.md`: English and Spanish narration, short presenter introduction, captions, and suggested photo order.
- `listing-data.json`: the same invented specifications in a reusable structured file.
- `IMAGE-PROMPTS.md`: the generation prompts and reference used for each image. Created with the built-in image-generation tool.

## Suggested manual assembly

1. Create a new project using manual entry. Leave the source-listing URL blank; there is no real listing to import.
2. Paste the title, fictional location, and description from your preferred language file.
3. Upload property photos 01–10 and retain their numbered order. Use 01 as the cover.
4. Choose a horizontal 16:9 video to preserve the composition. Start with about 4 seconds per photo and gentle movement. Longer narration may need longer scenes.
5. Use the broker portrait for an optional brief opening introduction. Use a stock/synthetic voice of your choice; no real broker's voice or identity is supplied.
6. Add the supplied narration and understated instrumental music available to your account. Keep narration clearly audible over the music.
7. End on the pool/garden view with “Create your next property video with VendeClip.” This demo should not invite viewers to book a real property viewing.
8. Include “AI-generated property and presenter · Fictional demo” in the final card or caption, then export your finished video.

The images share a design direction and reference exterior. They are illustrative views, not a measured architectural model or proof of an exact floor plan. The ten photos show selected spaces, not every room implied by the invented specifications.

This kit does not edit or replace the current landing-page video. Use the finished export to replace that example when your manual video is ready.
